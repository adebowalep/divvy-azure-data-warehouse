Divvy bike share · Chicago · 1 Feb 2021 – 31 Jan 2022

Divvy Ride Atlas

What 4.6 million trips and 1.9 million payments say about when, where and how long people ride, and what they pay. Built on a star schema in Azure Synapse. This page is a static snapshot with no live database behind it.

How the numbers were made. Every figure on this page was computed locally with Python from the project's source CSV files, using the same rules as the Azure Synapse warehouse (valid ride = 0 to 24 hours; age = completed years; rider type = the rider's current membership flag). The headline totals, trip-duration issues, age bands, spend by year, spend by age at account start and the usage-vs-spend bands were also observed in Synapse query results and match. The heatmap, monthly, map, flow, route, bike-mix and account views were not run in Synapse. The page does not connect to Azure. Ride data: City of Chicago Divvy trips. Rider, account and payment records are synthetic (course dataset). Membership is the current flag, not history, and payments are not linked to individual trips.
